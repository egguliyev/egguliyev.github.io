What is actually being simulated

Forward model. Phantoms are sums of analytic ellipses, so line integrals are exact: for each ellipse the Radon transform is closed-form, and path length is accumulated per material. Nothing is projected from a pixel grid, so the sinogram carries no discretization error of its own.

Spectrum. Five energy bins (40–120 keV) with 120 kVp fluence weights. Transmitted quanta per bin are N₀·w(E)·exp(−Σ μₘ(E)·Lₘ) with μ from tabulated NIST-like values for water, fat, lung, cortical bone, iodinated blood and titanium. The log-signal is formed from the summed bins, which is where beam hardening comes from — no artifact is painted on.

Detector. EID weights each bin by E and adds Gaussian electronic noise; PCD counts quanta with unity weight, has no electronic noise floor, and optionally spreads charge into neighbouring channels. At low dose the difference in CNR is the real one you would measure on a bench.

Artifacts. Scatter is added as an additive intensity floor before the log. Ring artifacts come from a fixed per-channel gain error. Motion displaces flagged structures as a function of view angle. Sparse views, photon starvation and metal streaks all emerge from the physics rather than from a filter.

Reconstruction. FBP uses an FFT ramp filter in cycles/cm with selectable apodization; the backprojection is in physical units, so the output is μ in cm⁻¹ and converts to HU against μwater(70 keV) = 0.195 cm⁻¹. SIRT uses a matched Joseph ray-driven projector pair with row/column normalization; the TV variant adds a gradient-descent step on total variation between iterations.

Metrics. Noise σ and CNR from the marked ROIs; RMSE, PSNR and SSIM against the ground truth rasterized on the same grid, evaluated only inside the patient and with HU mapped to [0,1] for SSIM (RMSE includes partial-volume differences at sharp edges, so it never reaches zero); cupping as centre-minus-periphery HU in the uniform region; MTF from an oversampled radial edge-spread function around the marked disk; NPS from the 2-D FFT of the background ROI. Attenuation values are approximate and intended for teaching and method comparison, not for dosimetry or acceptance testing.
