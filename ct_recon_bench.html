Acquisition geometry

Off = point sampling. On = 3-point average across each element, so pitch limits resolution as it does on a real module.

X-ray tube

Sets the bremsstrahlung endpoint. Above 70 kVp the tungsten K lines at 59 and 67 keV appear in the spectrum.
Self-absorption in the anode. Stationary in the detector frame, so it maps to rings — and because it hardens the beam on the anode side, a scalar flat-field does not fully remove it.
Uses a detector-weighted 20 cm water slab as the HU reference. This scalar calibration does not remove beam hardening or guarantee 0 HU throughout a reconstructed water cylinder.
Passes 70% of primary and 15% of scatter. Scatter is a deterministic additive field here; its quantum noise is not simulated.

Detector & exposure

A spatial redistribution of expected counts — the resolution cost of charge sharing, not its spectral physics. There is no pulse-height model here, so no shared-event energy loss, double counting, bin migration, pile-up or dead time.

Physics & artifacts

Off = the 70 keV reference model; sampling, noise and other artifacts still affect HU. On = beam hardening: cupping, dark bands, metal streaks.

What is actually being simulated

Scope. A 2D parallel-beam teaching and method-comparison model. SOD/SDD affect the approximate focal-spot response and beam profile; this is not a fan-beam or cone-beam scanner simulation.

Forward model. Analytic ellipse chords are accumulated by material. Aperture sub-rays remain separate through attenuation and their transmitted intensities are averaged. Angular sampling, three-point aperture quadrature, and the 1 keV energy grid remain finite approximations.

Source. An unvalidated Kramers spectrum with approximate tungsten lines, Al/Cu filtration, and a simple bowtie. The 1 keV spectrum is retained during transport to avoid collapsing energy intervals across detector K-edges. HVL uses air-kerma weighting. The heel gradient is placed across this 2D detector for illustration; it is not a realistic model of a scanner's longitudinal heel distribution. Focal-spot blur is a Gaussian approximation with isocentre FWHM f(M−1)/M.

Detector data. CsI and GOS use the NIST compound tables, including both sides of their K-edges. CZT assumes Cd₀.₉Zn₀.₁Te and mass-weighted NIST elemental coefficients. Log-log interpolation does not bridge edge discontinuities. CsI/GOS bulk densities are 4.51/7.44 g/cm³; CZT uses an assumed 5.78 g/cm³. Porosity, dopants and packaging are not represented.

Response. 1−exp(−μt) from total attenuation is an interaction-probability approximation; every interaction is then treated as a full-energy event. EID weights these events by energy and adds electronic noise; PCD weights them equally. Partial deposition, K-escape, scintillator light spread/conversion statistics, thresholds, energy resolution, pile-up, dead time and spectral charge sharing are absent. PCD cross-talk redistributes expected counts spatially; it does not reproduce shared-event covariance. Results do not establish a real detector ranking or quantitative bench CNR.

Exposure. Per-element mode specifies central incident quanta per view. Fixed-scan mode holds the total incident photon budget constant across channels/views, including bowtie/heel profiles. Relative-mAs mode also scales an approximate source yield with filtration and kVp. None is an absorbed-dose calculation or a calibrated absolute mAs setting.

Calibration and scatter. Air and object signals use the same spatial response. The HU reference uses a detector-weighted 20 cm water slab; it does not correct path-dependent beam hardening. The scatter slider sets pre-grid signal SPR on the most attenuated ray of each view. Grid transmission is 0.70 for primary and 0.15 for scatter. Scatter is a deterministic smooth field without its own quantum noise or energy spectrum.

Reconstruction and metrics. FBP uses a physical-unit ramp filter; SIRT uses a matched discrete projector/adjoint, with optional TV steps rather than a full statistical MBIR model. Monochromatic truth uses the legacy 70 keV reference (water 0.195 cm⁻¹); polychromatic reconstruction can differ because of energy dependence as well as reconstruction error. Single-ROI standard deviation/NPS include residual structure and artifacts. The disk-edge MTF is an illustrative estimate, especially with nonlinear denoising or coarse sampling. NPS units are HU²·mm². These are not acceptance-test or dosimetry results.

Sources. NIST SRD 126: CsI, GOS, Cd, Zn, Te, air, nominal compound densities. Source checked 30 September 2026. Object/filter attenuation tables and the tube spectrum remain approximate educational inputs.
